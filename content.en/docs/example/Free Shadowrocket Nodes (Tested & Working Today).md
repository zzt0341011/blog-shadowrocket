## 2. What Is Shadowrocket?

[Shadowrocket](https://shadowrocket.ink/zh) is a popular proxy client for iOS / iPadOS. It supports mainstream protocols including Shadowsocks (SS), ShadowsocksR (SSR), VMess, VLESS, and Trojan, and lets you get online by importing individual nodes or a subscription link.

![shadowrocket-fenxiang-1003.jpg](https://shadowrocket.ink/img/shadowrocket-fenxiang-1003.jpg)

It must be purchased and downloaded from the App Store using a **non-China Apple ID** (around USD 2.99). Android users can use alternative clients such as v2rayNG or Clash — the nodes on this site work with those too.

## 3. Shadowrocket Import Tutorial


**Importing a single link manually**

1. Copy any `ss://` / `vmess://` / `trojan://` link above.
2. Return to Shadowrocket. A prompt to "Add Node" will pop up automatically at the top of the home screen — tap to confirm.
3. Select that node and flip the switch at the top of the home screen to "On" to connect.

**Connection tip**: for routing mode, choose "Configuration" or "Rule-based" so domestic traffic goes direct and overseas traffic goes through the proxy — this is faster and saves data.
